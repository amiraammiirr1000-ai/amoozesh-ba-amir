# Third-party notices

## Openjam vocabulary data

This app includes the 504 Essential English Words headword list from [Openjam](https://github.com/amirj4m/openjam), including its 42 lesson groupings. Openjam identifies its dataset and code as MIT-licensed. The included list is stored in `504-essential-words.json`. Persian meanings, senses, examples and pronunciation metadata are retrieved from the Openjam public API and cached on the learner's device for offline use.

Openjam source: https://github.com/amirj4m/openjam
